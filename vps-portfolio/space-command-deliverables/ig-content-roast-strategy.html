IG Website Roast — Content Strategy

Faceless, high-engagement long-form content format built on pointing-stick trends and the "Day X until" hook pattern.

01 — INSTAGRAM BIO

Final Bio (129 chars, under 150 limit)

@spacecoastmarketing
your competitor's website shouldn't be
why they're beating you.

let's change that.

🚀 brevard county, fl
↓ free website roast

Why this bio works

  • "your competitor's website shouldn't be why they're beating you" — Names the injustice, not just the problem. It's specific pain.
  • "let's change that" — Grammatically correct call to action. "That" = the situation, not the competitor's site.
  • "free website roast" — Platform-native language. "Roast" = entertainment. "Audit" = homework. IG demographic 35/under responds to roast.
  • "brevard county" — Locals say "Brevard," not "Space Coast." More searchable, more authentic.
  • Zero corporate jargon — No "solutions," "optimize," "visibility." Pure IG energy.
02 — THE FORMAT

Faceless Website Roast Series

Core Mechanics

  • Camera: Pointed forward at a screen (never at face)
  • Pointer: Funny object used to gesture at errors on screen (spatula, toy dinosaur, back scratcher, banana, anything stupid)
  • Voiceover: Your real voice — no AI voice. Authenticity is the whole point.
  • Structure: Show 2-3 errors on target site → cut to your site showing the fix → repeat

The Hook

"Day X of roasting websites until there are no more bad websites"

This "Day X until Y" format is a proven engagement flywheel. People follow series. They submit. They check back. The algorithm pushes serial content harder than one-offs.

Why it works (5 reasons)

  1. Format is proven. Pointing stick + screen recording is already trending. Zero face, zero setup, just a funny object and voice.
  2. "Day X until Y" is engagement crack. People follow series. They submit. They check back. Every day = new algorithm push.
  3. It's a demo, not a pitch. You're not selling — you're showing. Viewer connects dots themselves. 10x more persuasive than "hire me."
  4. Feeds the bio. Bio says "free website roast" → content IS website roasts → viewer thinks "roast mine" → DM → lead.
  5. Infinite content supply. Bad websites will never run out.
03 — CRITICAL RULE

Do NOT Roast Brevard Businesses

⚠️

Roast national randoms, big brands, funny niche sites — never local.

The last thing you need is a local roofer seeing you dunk on his site and bad-mouthing you at the Chamber of Commerce. Keep it national/funny/absurd.

Safe Targets

  • Big brands with broken mobile (always funny, always safe)
  • Niche e-commerce sites with terrible UX
  • Restaurant chains with unusable menus
  • Government websites (universal punching bag)
  • Old corporate sites that haven't been touched since 2008
04 — FIRST BATCH IDEAS

10 Launch Roasts

  1. Day 1: A big restaurant chain with an unreadable mobile menu — show yours side by side. Hook: "Your menu is losing you money."
  2. Day 2: A local service business website from another state (safe distance) with no contact form above the fold.
  3. Day 3: Government website — municipal site from a random midwest town. Easy target.
  4. Day 4: E-commerce site with 8-second load time. Show LCP fail in real-time.
  5. Day 5: A dentist in California with a site from 2012. Before/after with your template.
  6. Day 6: "This website has NO mobile version in 2026." Find a desktop-only relic.
  7. Day 7: A real estate agent site with broken images everywhere.
  8. Day 8: "This law firm spent $8K on this." Find an overpriced, underperforming site.
  9. Day 9: CLS nightmare — point out every layout shift with increasingly ridiculous objects.
  10. Day 10: "Someone submitted their own website for a roast." (Make this one yourself to seed the trend — use a fake/placeholder site you built poorly on purpose.)
💡

Pro move: Seed the submissions

Around Day 7-8, mention "people have been DMing me their sites." Even if nobody has yet, saying it creates the behavior. Social proof is self-fulfilling.

05 — THE FUNNEL

Roast → Bio → DM → Lead

Every video ends with the same CTA: "Want yours roasted? Link in bio."

Bio drives to a landing page where they submit their URL. That page captures email. Email sequence sells the fix.

Landing Page for /roast

Create a page at spacecoastmarketingagency.com/roast that:

  • H1: "We'll Roast Your Website (Lovingly)"
  • Simple form: URL + email
  • Disclaimers: "Brevard County businesses get the roast privately — we never roast local."
  • Below form: CTA to book a strategy call for those who want the fix NOW
06 — SETUP

What You Need

  • Phone camera — pointed at your screen. Nothing fancy.
  • Funny pointer object — rotate through objects to keep it fresh (spatula, lightsaber, garden gnome, rubber chicken)
  • Screen recording — capture the target site and your site for the cutaway.
  • CapCut or DaVinci Resolve (free) for simple cuts.
  • Zero production value — that's the point. Over-produced = try-hard. Raw = authentic.
07 — SUCCESS METRICS

What to Track

  • Views per roast — benchmark Day 1, aim for growth each week
  • DMs received — how many people say "roast mine"
  • /roast page submissions — actual leads into the funnel
  • Follower growth — secondary metric, not the goal
  • Call bookings — the only metric that pays
📊

Realistic timeline

Expect nothing for 20-30 days of daily posting. Day 45+ is where the compound effect kicks in. This is a 100-day play minimum. Same as the Hormozi Rule of 100 for outreach — volume + consistency.
