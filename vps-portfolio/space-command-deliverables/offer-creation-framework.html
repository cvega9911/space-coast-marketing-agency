01 The Offer Design Philosophy

Three laws govern every high-converting offer architecture:

Law #1: Three Tiers Max

Choice paralysis kills conversion beyond three options. Science backs this — Hick's Law states decision time grows logarithmically with choices. Every tier beyond three dilutes attention and delays action.

Law #2: The Middle Must Be Obvious

The pricing anchor effect positions the middle tier as the no-brainer. When prospects compare, the middle tier's value-to-price ratio should dominate — making it feel like the smart choice, not the compromise.

Law #3: Name the Customer

Every tier must have a clear "who this is for." If you can't picture the exact customer standing in front of you when you describe a tier, the tier doesn't work. Vague tiers attract no one.

The Golden Rule

A confused mind never buys. Clarity is the highest-converting sales tool you own. If a prospect reads your tiers and asks "which one is right for me?" — you've already lost them.

03 The Pricing Psychology

Your prices aren't just numbers — they're signals that shape perception before a prospect reads a single feature.

Why This Price Architecture Works

⚓ Anchoring

Growth tier at $149/mo makes CMO at $1,500 feel premium and Website at $599 feel accessible. The middle sets the frame — everything else is measured against it.

🎯 Decoy Effect

Growth is intentionally close to Website in setup cost ($599 vs $599) but adds $149/mo. The prospect's brain asks: "why wouldn't I add the monthly?"

🛡️ Risk Reversal

72-hour preview guarantee + no contracts + client owns everything — removes the top three fears: will it look good? am I locked in? do I lose my site if I leave?

🔍 Price Transparency

Never hide prices. Visible pricing builds trust. Hidden pricing builds suspicion. Prospects who need to "call for pricing" assume they can't afford you — and leave.

🧠 The psychology of visible pricing: When you hide prices, you're telling prospects "you probably can't afford this." When you show them, you're saying "we're confident in our value and we respect your time." The second message converts.

04 The "Do-What-We-Said" Guarantee

"72-hour preview. Full delivery within 14 days. Or you don't pay."
No fine print. No exceptions. No awkward conversations.

Why This Works

  • Specific timeline: "72 hours" and "14 days" leave no room for ambiguity
  • Specific deliverable: "Preview" then "full delivery" — concrete milestones
  • Specific consequence: "Or you don't pay" — the risk is entirely ours

Why Vague Guarantees Fail

  • "Satisfaction guaranteed" — satisfaction of whom? By what standard?
  • "We'll make it right" — how? When? Define the mechanism
  • "100% money back" — of what? After what trigger?
The Trust Accelerator

A specific guarantee doesn't just reduce risk — it communicates competence. The precision of "72-hour preview" signals that you've done this many times before and know exactly how long it takes. That's more powerful than any testimonial.

06 How to Present Tiers in Sales Conversations

The way you talk about pricing is as important as the pricing itself. Here's the script.

🔑 Rule #1 — Lead With the Outcome, Not the Price

"Based on what you told me, I'd recommend our Growth tier — you get the full website plus ongoing social media and blog content that keeps your phone ringing."

🔑 Rule #2 — State the Price as a Fact, Then Pause

"The investment is $599 to build the site and $149 a month for the ongoing marketing."

→ Then stop talking. Let them respond first. The first person to speak after the price is stated loses negotiating leverage.

🔑 Rule #3 — The Growth Upsell from Website

"You're already investing $599 in the site. For $149 more per month, we handle everything — social media, blog posts, reviews. You focus on your business, we handle your presence."

🔑 Rule #4 — The CMO Upsell from Growth

"At $149/month, we're handling your online presence. For businesses ready to scale, our CMO retainer at $1,500 adds paid ads management, weekly strategy calls, and AI visibility optimization — essentially a full marketing department."

The Pause Is Your Secret Weapon

After stating the price, the silence feels uncomfortable — to them. They'll fill it with either an objection (which you can handle) or an acceptance. If you keep talking, you talk yourself out of the sale.

07 Common Pricing Mistakes

These are the seven pricing sins that kill conversion. Avoid them at all costs.

# Mistake Why It Fails The Fix
1 Too many tiers Analysis paralysis — prospects can't choose so they choose nothing Max 3 tiers. Kill any tier you can't articulate in one sentence.
2 Hiding prices Destroys trust before the conversation starts. "Call for pricing" = "you can't afford us" Show every price. Transparency signals confidence.
3 Discounting the premium tier Devalues the brand — if your best work is negotiable, none of your work is premium Hold firm on CMO pricing. The clients who demand discounts are the ones who don't value strategy.
4 No clear differentiation If prospects can't tell tiers apart, they default to the cheapest — or leave Each tier must have a distinct customer profile. If you can't name them, kill the tier.
5 Free hosting for life Long-term liability at scale — 100 clients × hosting costs = margin erosion Revisit at 20+ clients. Transition to "first year free" or bundle into maintenance plans.
6 No guarantee Fear of wasting money is the #1 unspoken objection. No guarantee = no safety net. 72-hour preview guarantee. Concrete, specific, risk-free.
7 Charging for the audit Kills top of funnel — every dollar you charge at discovery costs you 10x in lost pipeline Free audit, monetize the fix. The audit proves you can deliver. The implementation is what they pay for.
⚠️ Mistake #5 escalates fast: "Free hosting for life" sounds generous at 5 clients. At 50 clients, it's a $5,000+/year liability. At 200 clients, it's a line item that can sink your agency. Build expiration into your terms from day one.

08 The Audit as Offer Accelerant

Your free visibility audit isn't an add-on — it's the engine that makes all three tiers convert.

🔬 Step 1: Demonstrate Expertise First

Before asking for money, prove you see things they don't. The audit reveals blind spots — missing Google Business Profile optimization, zero AI visibility, competitor ranking above them. Expertise demonstrated is more powerful than expertise claimed.

⚡ Step 2: Create Undeniable Urgency

"Your competitor ranks above you on ChatGPT when someone asks for your service in your city." That's not a sales pitch — it's a fact you just handed them. Specific, verifiable, and impossible to ignore.

🔄 Step 3: Natural Transition to the Offer

"Want us to fix this?" — four words that follow the audit. No pitch needed. The audit did the pitching. You're simply asking if they want the problem solved by the people who found it.

The Audit Funnel Math

Free audit → 40-60% of recipients schedule a call → 30-50% of calls convert to a paid tier. The audit doesn't cost you leads — it creates them. Every minute spent on a free audit returns 5-10x in closed revenue.

🔗 The audit ties the entire framework together: Three tiers give prospects choice. The audit gives them reason. The guarantee removes risk. Together, they form a conversion system — not just a pricing page.
