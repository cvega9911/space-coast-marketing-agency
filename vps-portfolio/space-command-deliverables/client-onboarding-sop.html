Section 02

Phase 1 — Client Intake Hour 0 – 2

The single most important step. Garbage in, garbage out. Collect everything upfront so the agents never stall waiting for assets.

Intake Checklist

  • Business name, address, phone, email
  • Existing website URL (if any)
  • 3 – 5 competitor URLs they want to beat
  • Brand assets (logo, colors, fonts)
  • 5 – 10 photos of their business / work
  • Target keywords (3 – 5)
  • Current Google Business Profile login
  • Social media account URLs
  • What they like / dislike about their current site
  • Their top 3 services / products
📋

Pro Tip: Send the Intake Form Immediately

Have the intake form link ready before the contract is signed. Send it the moment the DocuSign returns. Every hour of delay pushes the preview back. Use Typeform or a Google Form — Socrates can read both.

If the client returns the form with fewer than 7 of the 10 checklist items, do not start the build. Send a polite follow-up: "I want to make sure we build exactly what you have in mind — could you fill in the missing pieces?" It protects quality and sets the standard for the relationship.

Section 04

Phase 3 — Client Preview Hour 48 – 72

The preview moment. Done right, it closes 80%+ of projects in a single round. Done wrong, it triggers endless revision spirals. Follow this script.

📧 The Preview Email Template

Send this email (or Slack message) exactly as written. It's been tested across 50+ clients.

Subject: Your new website is ready for preview 🚀 Hey [First Name], Your new site is live for preview: [Vercel Preview Link] We built a 5-page responsive site based on everything you shared — clean, fast, and optimized for [top keyword]. The design draws from the competitors you listed but positions you ahead of them with stronger calls-to-action and modern layout. Could you check these 3 things? 1. Mobile load — open it on your phone. Does it feel fast? 2. Your phone number — is it right everywhere? 3. The services list — did we capture your top 3? Then just reply with: • What works? • What needs changing? • Anything missing? We'll turn around edits in under 24 hours. — Chris

The Feedback Form (Keep It Simple)

✅ What works?

Ask this first. It anchors the conversation in positivity and tells you what to preserve when you make changes.

🔧 What needs changing?

The action items. Frame it as "changing" not "fixing" — subtle but powerful psychology.

➕ Anything missing?

Catches scope creep early. If they ask for something outside the package, you have a natural segue to the Growth or CMO tier.

How to Handle Feedback

Feedback Type Action Turnaround Agent
Minor edits Text changes, color tweaks, image swaps < 1 hour Mugen
Layout changes Section reorder, new component 4 – 8 hours Mugen + Qwen
Major changes New page, content overhaul, different design direction 24 hours Full pipeline re-run
Scope creep Feature requests outside original package N/A Escalate to Chris
🛑

Maximum 3 Revision Rounds

After round 3, pivot to a scope conversation: "We've incorporated a lot of great feedback — at this point, additional changes would fall under a new phase. Let's talk about what that looks like." This protects margins and prevents the "death by a thousand tweaks" trap.

Section 05

Phase 4 — Launch & Training Day 3 – 14

Go-live is not the finish line — it's the starting gun for the relationship. Nail these 9 steps and you'll earn a client for life (and a referral).

Go-Live Checklist

  • Domain connected and SSL verified — DNS propagates within minutes if set up correctly
  • Google Business Profile optimized — updated with new site URL, fresh photos, correct categories
  • Google Search Console submitted — sitemap.xml indexed; request initial crawl
  • Analytics set up — basic GA4 property with key events (form submit, phone click, CTA click)
  • Client trained on how to request changes — introduce them to the "ticket" system (even if it's just a Slack channel)
  • First blog post scheduled — queued in the CMS for publication within 7 days
  • Social media accounts connected — link icons verified; auto-publish configured if applicable
  • 30-day check-in scheduled — calendar invite sent: "How's the site performing? Anything we can tweak?"
  • Referral ask deployed — see below. This is not optional.

🗣️ The Referral Ask — Script

Send this within 48 hours of launch. 30% of your next clients will come from this single message.

"We loved building this for you. If you know any other business owners who need a website (or are frustrated with theirs), we'd be grateful for the intro. We offer a $___ referral credit for anyone you send our way."
🎯

Post-Launch Retention Principle

Every month your client's site sits unchanged is a month they question the value. Schedule the 30-day check-in before you leave the launch call. A client who hears from you proactively stays a client. A client who has to reach you first starts shopping competitors.

Section 06

Growth Tier — Additional Steps

For clients on the Growth tier ($2,500+), these steps layer on top of the Website tier build. Everything below is also agent-executed.

📄 10-Page Site

Add Portfolio, Testimonials, FAQ, Team, and Case Studies pages. Qwen drafts all five; Mugen integrates them into the design system with consistent navigation.

✍️ 2 Blog Posts (Month 1)

Scheduled for publication on Day 7 and Day 21. Topics are keyword-researched against competitor content. Written by Qwen, fact-checked by Gemma.

📱 30 Social Posts (Month 1)

Queued across the client's platforms — 1 per day for the first month. Mix of educational, promotional, and behind-the-scenes content.

⭐ Review Management Setup

Google review link generated and formatted for SMS/email. Automated review request flow configured (post-service trigger).

📊 Monthly Reporting Template

GA4 dashboard connected. Automated PDF report with: traffic, leads, keyword rankings, and a 1-paragraph executive summary drafted by agent.

Section 07

CMO Tier — Additional Steps

For clients on the CMO tier ($5,000+), this is where you stop being a web agency and start being their marketing department. Everything builds on the Growth tier.

📋 Competitor AEO Audit

Delivered as a branded PDF report. Kage analyzes 3–5 competitors and maps their Answer Engine Optimization gaps. Includes screenshots, keyword gap analysis, and an actionable priority list.

🔗 Backlink Outreach Campaign

Initialized within Week 1. First 20 prospects identified and contacted. Socrates manages the outreach sequence: research → draft → send → follow-up.

📈 Ad Account Audit & Setup

Via Madgicx. Existing ad accounts audited for wasted spend. New campaigns structured if needed. Pixel/conversion tracking verified.

📅 Weekly Call Scheduled

Recurring 30-minute calendar invite — same time every week. Agenda: wins, blockers, next 7 days. Socrates drafts a pre-call briefing 2 hours before.

📝 Weekly Executive Report

Template includes: traffic summary, lead count, keyword movement, social engagement, ad spend/ROAS, and 3 recommended actions for next week.

👥 Team Introductions

Position yourself as department head, not solo operator. Introduce agents by name (Qwen, Gemma, Mugen) as "specialists on the team." This builds perceived scale and justifies the retainer.

💡 The Positioning Shift

At the CMO tier, you're not selling a website. You're selling "a marketing department for less than the cost of one junior hire." Every deliverable reinforces that narrative. The agents are your team. You're the director.

Section 08

The Handoff to Socrates

This is the operational breakthrough. After intake, Chris's involvement drops to near-zero until the preview link is delivered. Here's how the workload splits:

Socrates & Agents: 85% of the work Chris: 15% (3 checkpoints)

✅ Chris Checkpoint 1

Intake Form Review
Review the completed form before releasing it to Socrates. Verify: all 10 checklist items present, brand assets attached, competitor URLs look reasonable. Takes 5–10 minutes.

✅ Chris Checkpoint 2

Preview Approval
Review the Vercel preview yourself before sending it to the client. Open on mobile, click every link, read the copy. If anything feels off, flag it to Socrates for a one-shot fix. Takes 10–15 minutes.

✅ Chris Checkpoint 3

Launch Call
30-minute Zoom with the client. Walk through the site, demonstrate how to request changes, set expectations for ongoing communication, and deliver the referral ask. That's it.
