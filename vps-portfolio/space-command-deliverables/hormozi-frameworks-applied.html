Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)
↑ Maximize the numerator. ↓ Drive the denominator to zero. That's how you create irresistible offers.

Why This Works — Hormozi's Criteria Check

✓ Cannot Be Compared — No Brevard agency offers 72-hour AI-powered preview with no contracts. You can't comparison-shop something that doesn't exist anywhere else.
✓ Premium Price — $149/mo positions above freelancers (who charge $15–50/hr with no strategy) and below enterprise agencies ($3K–10K/mo). It's the "sweet spot" Hormozi describes.

01D. The 5 Enhancements Applied

Hormozi's five levers for transforming any offer into an irresistible one — mapped directly to SCM:

#EnhancementHormozi's DefinitionSCM Application
1 Scarcity Limit supply to increase perceived value "We only take X clients per month to ensure quality." Must be genuine — if you're a solo operator with AI assistance, capacity is legitimately limited. Never fabricate scarcity.
2 Urgency Give a reason to act now, not later "72-hour preview delivery" creates natural urgency. "If you sign up today, you'll see your site by Friday." No manufactured deadlines — the speed itself is the urgency driver.
3 Bonuses Add items that increase value without proportionally increasing cost Free GBP optimization with every website. Free visibility audit with every consultation. These are AI-generated, near-zero marginal cost, but high perceived value.
4 Guarantees Reverse the risk — make it safer for them to buy than to walk away "72-hour preview or you don't pay." Specific (72 hours). Measurable (preview delivered or not). Meaningful (they keep their money). Far superior to vague "satisfaction guaranteed."
5 Naming Use the [Result] + [Time Period] formula "5-Page Website in 72 Hours — Guaranteed" follows Hormozi's exact naming convention. "Free Visibility Audit — Delivered in 48 Hours" also fits the formula.
