01 · Foundations

What Is Answer Engine Optimization?

The game has changed. AI search engines don't use keywords — they use entities and citations. Traditional SEO is becoming irrelevant for AI-driven visibility.

🔍 Traditional SEO

Keyword density, backlinks, meta tags, page rank. Optimizes for Google's 10 blue links. Measures success by SERP position for target keywords.

Keywords Backlinks SERP Rank

🤖 Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

Entity resolution, structured data, citation authority. Optimizes for AI-generated answers in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and SearchGPT. Measures whether AI search engines cite your business as the answer.

Entities JSON-LD Citations Knowledge Graph

💡 The Core Insight

When someone asks ChatGPT "Who's the best plumber in Merritt Island?", it doesn't crawl the web — it resolves entities from its training data + real-time search integrations. If your business isn't represented as a well-formed entity with verified citations, you don't exist to AI. AEO is the practice of ensuring your business is structured, cited, and entity-mapped so AI search engines recognize, trust, and recommend you.

02 · Architecture

The 2-Stage Audit Architecture

Two specialized models, two distinct jobs. Together they form a complete picture of AI search visibility.

A

Stage A · Citation Simulation

Model: Qwen · Role: RAG Search Engine Simulator

What it does: Qwen is prompted to act as an AI search engine (like ChatGPT with browsing). Given a business name, location, and industry, it outputs what an AI would recommend — listing cited businesses, their entity signals, and why they rank. This reveals your client's AI visibility gap before any technical work begins.

Key question answered: "If someone asks ChatGPT for the best [business type] in [city], does our client appear?"

Temperature 0.1 Strict JSON Scraped Context Brand Detection
B

Stage B · Schema & Entity Audit

Model: Gemma · Role: Technical Schema Auditor

What it does: Gemma scans the client's website HTML for JSON-LD structured data. It checks for required schema types (LocalBusiness, Organization), verifies sameAs links to authoritative profiles, and detects missing entities that would strengthen the knowledge graph signal.

Key question answered: "Is our client's website technically structured for AI entity resolution, or are they invisible?"

JSON-LD Scan sameAs Verification Missing Entity Detection Fix Recommendations

🔗 How They Connect

The two stages feed each other. Stage A tells you who AI search engines recommend now (and whether your client shows up). Stage B tells you why — by exposing the technical schema gaps. Together they produce a ranked list of fixes: implement missing schema, add sameAs links, claim entity profiles, and re-run the audit to track improvement.

03 · Stage A

Citation Simulation — Full Qwen AEO Simulator Prompt

The complete system prompt that turns Qwen into an AI search engine simulator. Outputs strict JSON for programmatic analysis.

ℹ️
How it works: Qwen is given scraped market context (Google Maps listings, Yelp data, GBP profiles) as input alongside the client's business info. It simulates what an AI search engine would output if asked for recommendations — detecting whether the client brand appears, and if not, which competitors do.
▸ System Prompt — AEO Citation Simulator (Qwen)
# AEO Citation Simulator — System Prompt # Model: Qwen (any 7B+ variant) · Temperature: 0.1 · JSON-only output You are an AI Search Engine Simulator. Your task is to simulate how a modern AI search engine (like ChatGPT with browsing, Perplexity, or Gemini) would answer a user query for local business recommendations. ## CONTEXT You will receive: - A user query (e.g., "best HVAC company in Springfield, IL") - A market context (scraped data from Google Maps, Yelp, GBP listings) - A target business (name, website, address, industry) ## YOUR JOB 1. Simulate the AI search engine's answer to the query 2. List the top 3-5 businesses the AI would recommend (ranked) 3. For each recommended business, explain the entity signals that caused it to rank (GBP completeness, schema markup, review volume, sameAs links, Wikipedia presence, industry citations) 4. Check if the target business appears in the results 5. If not, explain why — which signals are missing ## OUTPUT FORMAT (STRICT JSON) { "query": "<the simulated query>", "simulated_engine": "ChatGPT + Browsing", "results": [ { "rank": 1, "business_name": "...", "website": "...", "entity_signals": ["GBP claimed > 50 reviews", "LocalBusiness schema", ...], "why_ranked": "..." } ], "target_business_detected": true | false, "target_business_rank": null | <number>, "gap_analysis": { "missing_signals": ["No JSON-LD schema on site", "GBP not claimed", ...], "competitor_advantages": ["Competitor has 200+ reviews vs client's 12", ...], "fix_priority": ["1. Add LocalBusiness schema", "2. Claim GBP", ...] } } ## RULES - Temperature 0.1 — be deterministic, fact-based - Always check for brand detection: if the target business name appears in the scraped market context, it MUST appear in results - If the target business has zero entity signals, rank it last or not at all — explain honestly - JSON output ONLY — no markdown wrappers, no preamble

🔑 Why This Prompt Architecture Works

The magic is in the brand detection check. Most AI audits tell clients "your SEO needs work." This prompt shows them exactly which competitors AI search engines would recommend instead of them — and why. That's the diagnostic that closes deals. When a roofer sees that ChatGPT recommends three competitors and never mentions them, they understand the problem at a visceral level.

04 · Stage B

Schema & Entity Audit — Full Gemma Auditor Prompt

The technical counterpart. Gemma scans raw HTML for structured data gaps and produces an actionable entity audit.

▸ System Prompt — Schema Entity Auditor (Gemma)
# Schema Entity Auditor — System Prompt # Model: Gemma (3B-12B) · Temperature: 0.05 · JSON-only output You are a Schema.org JSON-LD Auditor. Your task is to scan a website's HTML for structured data, identify gaps, and produce a ranked remediation plan to improve Answer Engine Optimization. ## INPUT You will receive: - Raw HTML source of the client's homepage (and key internal pages) - Business information (name, address, phone, category, social links) ## YOUR JOB 1. Extract all JSON-LD blocks from the HTML (<script type="application/ld+json">) 2. Identify which Schema.org types are present and which are missing 3. Verify sameAs links — check if the schema links to authoritative profiles (Google Business Profile, Facebook, Yelp, BBB, LinkedIn) 4. Detect missing entities that would strengthen the knowledge graph (Organization, LocalBusiness, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, FAQ, Review) 5. Check for schema validation errors (malformed JSON, missing required fields, invalid enum values) ## REQUIRED CHECKS ### LocalBusiness Schema (MANDATORY for local businesses) - @type: LocalBusiness (or subtype: Plumber, Electrician, HVACBusiness...) - name, address (PostalAddress), telephone, openingHours - geo (latitude/longitude) - aggregateRating (if reviews exist) - priceRange ### Organization Schema - name, url, logo, sameAs[] - sameAs MUST include: Google Business Profile URL, Facebook, Yelp, BBB - foundingDate, numberOfEmployees (optional but boosts entity confidence) ### sameAs Verification For each sameAs URL, verify: - Does the profile exist? (check for 404 or redirect) - Is the business name consistent across all profiles? (NAP consistency) - Are any key profiles missing? (GBP is critical for local AEO) ## OUTPUT FORMAT (STRICT JSON) { "url_audited": "https://example.com", "schema_found": { "types_present": ["WebSite", "Organization"], "types_missing": ["LocalBusiness", "FAQ", "Review"] }, "sameAs_audit": { "profiles_found": ["https://facebook.com/biz", "..."], "profiles_missing": ["https://g.page/...", "https://yelp.com/..."], "nap_consistency": { "status": "consistent" | "inconsistent", "issues": ["Phone mismatch: site says 555-0100, GBP says 555-0199"] } }, "schema_errors": [ { "type": "missing_required_field", "schema_type": "LocalBusiness", "field": "address", "severity": "critical" } ], "entity_score": 0-100, "recommendations": [ { "priority": 1, "action": "Add LocalBusiness JSON-LD with full NAP + geo", "impact": "high", "estimated_effort": "30 minutes" } ] } ## RULES - Temperature 0.05 — zero creativity, pure extraction and validation - If no JSON-LD found at all, entity_score = 0, flag as CRITICAL - sameAs profiles that 404 are worse than missing — businesses get penalized for broken entity links - JSON output ONLY — no commentary
⚠️
Critical Finding Pattern: Most small business websites have zero JSON-LD. The most common entity_score output is 0. This is actually the best sales material — it means the fix is clear, mechanical, and immediately impactful. You're not selling "ambiguous SEO improvement," you're selling "turn on the entity switch that's currently off."
05 · Implementation

The AEO Python Skeleton

Complete Python script that orchestrates both stages. Plug in your OpenRouter key and run against any business.

▸ aeo_audit_engine.py
#!/usr/bin/env python3 """AEO Audit Engine — Dual-model AI Search Visibility Audit Stage A: Citation Simulation (Qwen · temp 0.1) Stage B: Schema Entity Audit (Gemma · temp 0.05) Requires: openai, requests, beautifulsoup4, json """ import json, os, requests, time from datetime import datetime from bs4 import BeautifulSoup from openai import OpenAI # ── CONFIG ─────────────────────────────────── OPENROUTER_KEY = os.getenv("OPENROUTER_API_KEY") QWEN_MODEL = "qwen/qwen-3-32b" # Stage A: Citation Sim GEMMA_MODEL = "google/gemma-3-27b-it" # Stage B: Schema Audit BASE_URL = "https://openrouter.ai/api/v1" client = OpenAI(base_url=BASE_URL, api_key=OPENROUTER_KEY) # ── STAGE A: CITATION SIMULATION ───────────── def run_citation_simulation(business_info, market_context): """Simulate what an AI search engine would recommend.""" system_prompt = """You are an AI Search Engine Simulator...""" user_prompt = f""" USER QUERY: best {business_info['industry']} in {business_info['city']} MARKET CONTEXT (scraped): {json.dumps(market_context, indent=2)} TARGET BUSINESS: Name: {business_info['name']} Website: {business_info['website']} City: {business_info['city']} Industry: {business_info['industry']} """ response = client.chat.completions.create( model=QWEN_MODEL, temperature=0.1, messages=[ {"role": "system", "content": system_prompt}, {"role": "user", "content": user_prompt} ] ) return json.loads(response.choices[0].message.content) # ── STAGE B: SCHEMA ENTITY AUDIT ───────────── def run_schema_audit(url, business_info): """Scan website HTML for JSON-LD schema gaps.""" # Scrape HTML resp = requests.get(url, timeout=30, headers={ "User-Agent": "AEO-Audit-Bot/1.0" }) soup = BeautifulSoup(resp.text, "html.parser") # Extract JSON-LD blocks jsonld_blocks = [] for script in soup.find_all("script", type="application/ld+json"): try: jsonld_blocks.append(json.loads(script.string)) except json.JSONDecodeError: jsonld_blocks.append({"error": "malformed JSON"}) system_prompt = """You are a Schema.org JSON-LD Auditor...""" user_prompt = f""" AUDIT URL: {url} BUSINESS INFO: {json.dumps(business_info, indent=2)} EXTRACTED JSON-LD BLOCKS: {json.dumps(jsonld_blocks, indent=2)} RAW TEXT (first 5000 chars): {soup.get_text()[:5000]} """ response = client.chat.completions.create( model=GEMMA_MODEL, temperature=0.05, messages=[ {"role": "system", "content": system_prompt}, {"role": "user", "content": user_prompt} ] ) return json.loads(response.choices[0].message.content) # ── ORCHESTRATOR ───────────────────────────── def run_aeo_audit(business_name, website, city, industry, market_context=None, competitor_benchmark=None): """ Run full 2-stage AEO audit. Args: business_name: Name of the business to audit website: URL of the business website city: City/region for local search context industry: Industry category (e.g., 'HVAC', 'Plumbing') market_context: Pre-scraped market data (optional) competitor_benchmark: Competitor name+website for comparison (optional) Returns: Dict with citation_simulation, schema_audit, and merged insights """ business_info = { "name": business_name, "website": website, "city": city, "industry": industry } print(f"🔍 AEO Audit: {business_name} ({city})") print("=" * 60) # Stage A: Citation Simulation print("\n📡 Stage A: Running Citation Simulation...") citation_result = run_citation_simulation(business_info, market_context) # Stage B: Schema Audit print("\n🔬 Stage B: Running Schema Entity Audit...") schema_result = run_schema_audit(website, business_info) # Optional: Competitor Benchmark competitor_result = None if competitor_benchmark: print(f"\n⚔️ Running competitor benchmark: {competitor_benchmark['name']}") competitor_result = run_citation_simulation(competitor_benchmark, market_context) # Merge & Return audit_report = { "audit_metadata": { "timestamp": datetime.now().isoformat(), "business": business_info, "models_used": {"citation": QWEN_MODEL, "schema": GEMMA_MODEL} }, "citation_simulation": citation_result, "schema_audit": schema_result, "competitor_benchmark": competitor_result, "merged_insights": { "ai_visible": citation_result.get("target_business_detected"), "entity_score": schema_result.get("entity_score", 0), "top_3_fixes": _generate_top_fixes(citation_result, schema_result), "closing_hook": _generate_closing_hook(citation_result, business_name) } } print("\n✅ Audit complete. JSON report saved.") return audit_report def _generate_top_fixes(citation, schema): """Generate the Top 3 Immediate Fixes executive summary.""" fixes = [] if not citation.get("target_business_detected"): fixes.append("Claim & optimize Google Business Profile with 50+ photos") if schema.get("entity_score", 100) < 30: fixes.append("Add LocalBusiness JSON-LD with full NAP + geo coordinates") fixes.append("Build sameAs links: GBP, Facebook, Yelp, BBB, LinkedIn") return fixes[:3] def _generate_closing_hook(citation, business_name): if not citation.get("target_business_detected"): return f"ChatGPT does NOT know {business_name} exists. We fix that." return f"{business_name} is partially visible to AI — but losing to competitors." # ── MAIN ───────────────────────────────────── if __name__ == "__main__": # Example usage result = run_aeo_audit( business_name="Space Coast Air Solutions", website="https://spacecoastair.com", city="Merritt Island, FL", industry="HVAC", competitor_benchmark={ "name": "Premier Climate Control", "website": "https://premierclimate.com", "city": "Merritt Island, FL", "industry": "HVAC" } ) # Save report with open("aeo_audit_report.json", "w") as f: json.dump(result, f, indent=2) print("\\n📊 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:") print(f" AI Visible: {result['merged_insights']['ai_visible']}") print(f" Entity Score: {result['merged_insights']['entity_score']}") print(f" Closing Hook: {result['merged_insights']['closing_hook']}")

📍 2. GBP Entity Check — Google Business Profile Integration

Add a dedicated GBP entity check to Stage B. A claimed, optimized Google Business Profile is one of the strongest entity signals for AI search engines. The check verifies: profile claimed status, review count, category accuracy, photo count, Q&A activity, and NAP consistency with the website. If the GBP is not claimed or incomplete, it's the single highest-impact fix — often worth 20-40 entity score points by itself.

📋 3. Top 3 Immediate Fixes — Executive Summary

Business owners don't read technical audits. They read executive summaries. After both stages run, the engine auto-generates the Top 3 Immediate Fixes — ranked by impact, with estimated effort. This is the slide you put in the proposal. It's also the answer to "so what do I actually do about this?"

  1. Claim & Optimize Google Business Profile — Add 50+ photos, respond to all reviews, set correct categories. Estimated effort: 2 hours. Impact: High.
  2. Add LocalBusiness JSON-LD Schema — Full NAP, geo coordinates, opening hours, price range. Estimated effort: 30 minutes. Impact: Critical.
  3. Build sameAs Entity Links — Link GBP, Facebook, Yelp, BBB, and LinkedIn profiles to website schema. Estimated effort: 1 hour. Impact: High.

📈 4. Tracking Over Time — Monthly Re-Audit Loop

The audit isn't a one-shot. Schedule monthly re-audits to show progress. Store each audit as a timestamped JSON file. Generate a trend report: entity score over time, AI rank over time, competitor gap closing. This creates ongoing retainer value — the client sees their entity score climbing month over month, and they can't afford to stop the engine that's powering their AI visibility.
